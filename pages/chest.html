🏋️ Chest Workouts

Sculpt upper, middle, and lower pectorals with compound pressing movements.

🔍
Overall Chest

Flat Barbell Bench Press

🎯 Pectoralis Major🔄 4 Sets x 8 Reps

Grip bar slightly wider than shoulder-width, lower bar to mid-chest, press upwards forcefully.

Upper Chest

Incline Dumbbell Press

🎯 Clavicular Head🔄 3 Sets x 10 Reps

Set bench to 30-45 degrees, press dumbbells overhead bringing them together at the peak.

Chest Stretch

Flat Dumbbell Chest Flyes

🎯 Outer Pectorals🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Slightly bend elbows, lower dumbbells out to sides in an arc motion until chest stretches.

Lower Chest

Decline Barbell Bench Press

🎯 Sternal Head🔄 3 Sets x 10 Reps

Lie on decline bench, lower bar to lower chest area, press upwards engaging lower pec fibers.

Bodyweight

Bodyweight Push-Ups

🎯 Chest & Triceps🔄 3 Sets x 15 Reps

Maintain rigid plank posture, lower chest until elbows bend to 90 degrees, press body up.
