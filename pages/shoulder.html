🏋️ Shoulder Workouts

Develop 3D 3-head deltoids with overhead pressing and lateral raises.

🔍
Overall Shoulders

Seated Dumbbell Shoulder Press

🎯 Anterior & Lateral Deltoids🔄 4 Sets x 10 Reps

Press dumbbells vertically overhead until arms fully lock out, lower smoothly to ear height.

Side Delts

Dumbbell Lateral Raise

🎯 Lateral Deltoid🔄 3 Sets x 15 Reps

Raise dumbbells outward to shoulder height with a slight bend in elbows, controlling the descent.

Front Delts

Barbell Front Raise

🎯 Anterior Deltoid🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Raise barbell forward up to eye level, pause briefly, then lower with control.

Rear Delts

Rear Delt Flye

🎯 Posterior Deltoid🔄 3 Sets x 15 Reps

Hinge forward or use pec flye machine in reverse, pull hands outward contracting rear deltoids.

Traps

Heavy Barbell Shrug

🎯 Upper Trapezius🔄 4 Sets x 12 Reps

Hold heavy barbell, elevate shoulders straight up toward ears, hold contraction, lower smoothly.
