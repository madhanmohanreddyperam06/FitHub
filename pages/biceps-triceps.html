💪 Biceps & Triceps Workouts

Target long and short heads of biceps and triceps for balanced arm hypertrophy.

🔍
Biceps

Standing Barbell Bicep Curl

🎯 Biceps Brachii🔄 3 Sets x 10-12 Reps

Stand tall with shoulders back, curl barbell toward chest squeezing biceps at the peak.

Brachialis

Dumbbell Hammer Curl

🎯 Brachialis & Forearms🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Hold dumbbells with neutral grip (palms facing each other), curl upward maintaining elbow position.

Isolated Biceps

EZ-Bar Preacher Curl

🎯 Short Head Biceps🔄 3 Sets x 10 Reps

Rest upper arms against preacher pad, lower bar under full control, curl up smoothly.

Triceps

Cable Tricep Rope Pushdown

🎯 Triceps Lateral Head🔄 3 Sets x 12-15 Reps

Push rope attachment down toward thighs, spreading ends outward at full extension.

Long Head Triceps

Overhead Dumbbell Extension

🎯 Triceps Long Head🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Hold dumbbell overhead with both hands, lower behind head until elbows stretch, press back up.
