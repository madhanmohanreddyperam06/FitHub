🏋️ Leg Workouts

Build powerful quadriceps, hamstrings, glutes, and calves.

Quads & Glutes

Barbell Back Squat

🎯 Quadriceps & Glutes🔄 4 Sets x 8-10 Reps

Keep chest high, descend smoothly until thighs reach parallel, drive up through heels.

Quadriceps

45-Degree Leg Press

🎯 Quads & Hamstrings🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Place feet shoulder-width apart, unlock safety sled, lower weight to 90 degrees, press up.

Leg Stability

Dumbbell Walking Lunges

🎯 Quads & Glutes🔄 3 Sets x 12 Steps/Leg

Step forward into lunge posture lowering back knee toward ground, step through to next leg.

Hamstrings

Lying Hamstring Leg Curl

🎯 Hamstrings🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Lie face down, flex knees bringing leg pad toward glutes, lower under control.

Calves

Standing Calf Raises

🎯 Gastrocnemius🔄 4 Sets x 15 Reps

Stand on platform edge, lower heels for full stretch, press up onto tiptoes for peak contraction.
