🏋️ Back Workouts

Build a thick, V-tapered back with these targeted lat and row exercises.

Lats

Wide-Grip Lat Pulldown

🎯 Lats & Upper Back🔄 4 Sets x 10 Reps

Grip bar wider than shoulders, pull down toward upper chest squeezing your lats at the bottom.

Mid Back

Seated Cable Row

🎯 Rhomboids & Lats🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps

Pull handle toward abdomen while retracting shoulder blades, keeping core tight.

Upper Back

Bent Over Barbell Row

🎯 Lats & Traps🔄 3 Sets x 8-10 Reps

Hinge at hips with flat back, pull barbell to lower chest while keeping elbows tucked.

Lats

Single-Arm Dumbbell Row

🎯 Latissimus Dorsi🔄 3 Sets x 12 Reps / Arm

Support body on bench, pull dumbbell to hip level squeezing lat muscle.

Lower Back

Lower Back Hyperextension

🎯 Erector Spinae🔄 3 Sets x 15 Reps

Anchor legs on hyperextension bench, lower upper body and extend back up to neutral alignment.
